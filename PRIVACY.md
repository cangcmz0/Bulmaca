Veri sorumlusu, Google Play'de Patikaç'ı yayınlayan geliştiricidir (“biz”). İletişim: **Gocmez@outlook.com.tr**

### 2. Bizim topladığımız veriler
Oyun hesap açtırmaz; adınızı, e-postanızı, telefon numaranızı, fotoğraflarınızı, rehberinizi veya konum izninizi istemez. Oyun ilerlemeniz (seviye, yıldızlar, ipuçları, satın alınan ürünler, ayarlar) **yalnızca cihazınızda** saklanır; bize ya da bir sunucuya gönderilmez. Bizim sunucumuz yoktur. Oyunu kaldırdığınızda bu veriler silinir. Android'in otomatik yedeklemesi açıksa bu yerel veriler Google hesabınızın yedeğine dahil olabilir.

- **Kişiselleştirilmiş reklamlar:** açık rızanız (KVKK m.5/1; GDPR m.6/1-a). AB/BK/İsviçre'de rızanız Google'ın onay formuyla alınır; vermezseniz yalnızca kişiselleştirilmemiş reklamlar gösterilir.
- **Kişiselleştirilmemiş reklamlar, dolandırıcılığın ve kötüye kullanımın önlenmesi, güvenlik:** meşru menfaat (KVKK m.5/2-f; GDPR m.6/1-f).
- **Satın aldığınız ürünlerin teslimi ve geri yüklenmesi:** sözleşmenin kurulması ve ifası (KVKK m.5/2-c; GDPR m.6/1-b).

### 5. Uygulama izinleri
Oyun yalnızca şu izinleri kullanır: **İnternet** ve **ağ durumu** (reklamlar ve satın almalar için), **titreşim** (dokunma geri bildirimi), **reklam kimliği** ve Android **reklam hizmetleri** (reklam ölçümü), **Google Play faturalandırma** (satın almalar). Kamera, mikrofon, konum, rehber, depolama veya SMS izni **kullanılmaz**.

### 6. Reklam tercihleriniz
- Oyun içinde **Ayarlar → Reklam gizlilik seçenekleri** bölümünden onayınızı istediğiniz zaman değiştirebilir veya geri alabilirsiniz (AB/BK/İsviçre).
- Android **Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Reklamlar** bölümünden reklam kimliğinizi sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsiniz.
- Reklamsız ürünü satın alarak zorunlu (geçiş) reklamları kaldırabilirsiniz; ödüllü reklamlar her zaman isteğe bağlıdır.
- Reklamlar Google'ın en fazla **“Ebeveyn rehberliği” (PG)** içerik derecesiyle sınırlandırılmıştır.

### 8. Saklama süresi
Cihazınızdaki oyun verileri, oyunu kaldırana kadar saklanır. Google'ın işlediği veriler, Google'ın saklama politikalarına göre saklanır. Biz oyunculara ait hiçbir veriyi kendi tarafımızda saklamayız.

Ayrıca **Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu**'na veya AB'deki yetkili veri koruma otoritesine şikâyette bulunma hakkınız saklıdır.

## English

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game “Patikaç” (the “Game”) processes personal data and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”), Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”), the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (“COPPA”), the California Consumer Privacy Act (“CCPA/CPRA”) and the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

### 1. Controller
The controller is the developer who publishes Patikaç on Google Play (“we”). Contact: **Gocmez@outlook.com.tr**

### 2. Data we collect
The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location permission. Your progress (levels, stars, hints, purchased items, settings) is stored **only on your device** and is never sent to us or to any server. We run no servers. Uninstalling the Game deletes it. If Android auto backup is on, this local data may be included in your Google account backup.

### 3. Third-party services
The Game uses the following Google services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

| Service | Purpose | Data that may be processed |
|---|---|---|
| **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK) | Serving and measuring ads | Advertising ID and app set ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions (impressions, clicks, video views), device and app information, diagnostics (crashes, performance) |
| **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** | Collecting and storing ad consent in the EEA/UK/Switzerland | Consent choices, device information |
| **Google Play Billing** | Processing in-app purchases | Purchase records (by Google) |

Links: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) · [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) · [Google ad settings](https://myadcenter.google.com)

### 4. Purposes and legal bases
- **Personalised ads:** your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1)). In the EEA/UK/Switzerland consent is requested through Google's consent form; if you decline, only non-personalised ads are shown.
- **Non-personalised ads, fraud and abuse prevention, security:** legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
- **Delivering and restoring items you buy:** performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).

### 5. App permissions
The Game uses only: **Internet** and **network state** (ads and purchases), **vibration** (haptic feedback), **advertising ID** and Android **ad services** (ad measurement), and **Google Play billing** (purchases). It does **not** use camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or SMS permissions.

### 6. Your ad choices
- In the Game, **Settings → Ad privacy options** lets you change or withdraw consent at any time (EEA/UK/Switzerland).
- In Android **Settings → Privacy → Ads** you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
- Buying Remove Ads removes forced (interstitial) ads; rewarded ads are always optional.
- Ads are limited to Google's **“Parental guidance” (PG)** content rating or lower.

### 7. International transfers
Because of how Google's services work, ad and purchase data may be transferred to servers outside the EEA and Türkiye. These transfers rely on Google's standard contractual clauses and safeguards, in line with the GDPR and KVKK Art. 9.

### 8. Retention
Game data on your device is kept until you uninstall the Game. Data processed by Google is retained under Google's retention policies. We keep no player data on our side.

### 9. Children and families
The Game is **not directed at children under 13** and is published on Google Play for a target audience of 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (COPPA). The Game is not part of Google Play's “Designed for Families” programme. If a parent believes their child shared data through the Game, they can contact us and we will ask Google to delete the relevant data and take the necessary steps.

### 10. Your rights (GDPR and KVKK)
You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time and to seek compensation. Send requests to **Gocmez@outlook.com.tr**; we reply within **30 days**. As we hold no player data, you can also use Google's tools for ad data. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority or, in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Kurulu).

### 11. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)
We do not sell personal information for money. Our ad partner Google may use the advertising ID for personalised ads, which may count as “sharing” under the CCPA. You can stop this by deleting your advertising ID or turning off personalisation. You can exercise your rights to know and to delete via the email above, and you will not be discriminated against for doing so.

### 12. Security
Data is transmitted by Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The Game keeps its data only in its own app storage on your device.

### 13. Changes
We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced on this page and with an app update; the effective date is shown above.
