# Mağaza sayfası metinleri

Play Console → **Büyüme → Mağaza varlığı → Ana mağaza girişi**. Varsayılan dil İngilizce (en-US) olsun; Türkçe'yi "Çeviri ekle" ile ekle.

`Free the cats in the right order! 40,000 cute, brain-teasing puzzle levels.`

**Full description:**

```
Every cat looks one way. Tap it and it runs that way – unless another cat is in the path. Bump into each other and you lose a life. Find the right order and free them all!

Sounds easy? Wait until the spinners show up.

🌀 SPINNER CATS turn 90° every time ANY cat escapes. Free the wrong cat first and they lock up – you're cornered. Think one move ahead!
😴 SLEEPY CATS hide which way they face until you tap them awake.
😰 SCAREDY CATS run backwards, away from where they look. Your brain will want to argue.

★ 40,000 hand-tuned levels that get harder all the way, and Master Mode after that
★ Every level is guaranteed solvable
★ Hard and Boss levels against the clock
★ Daily Puzzle with streaks
★ Smart hints that always find a winning move
★ Cornered? Undo your wrong move and try again
★ Combos, flawless finishes and a lot of very cute cats
★ Plays offline, one hand, in short or long sessions

Free to play with optional ads.
```
